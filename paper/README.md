# Papers & Results

This directory contains the core outputs of the LLMagogyV2 research:

- **`LLMagogyV2.pdf`** — Full research report, including:
  - Hypothesis and motivation
  - Experimental design (10 training stages)
  - Detailed results and analysis
  - Conclusions and future work

- **`LLMagogyV2.xlsx`** — Raw numerical results from all experiments:
  - Training/validation loss over time
  - Duration, iterations, and hyperparameters per stage
  - Comparison across strategies (warmup, weight freeze, etc.)

> 🔍 The paper explains why staged training on non-repetitive data fails, and why progressive growth on full data succeeds.
